About Me

I am at the beginning of my web development journey, taking my first steps towards a much bigger goal. My interest in technology and problem-solving goes back to an early age, when I started exploring ways to create things, experiment with tools, and solve real-world problems through technology.

At the age of 13, I began repairing software-related issues for customers at my father's mobile shop, learning practical skills such as booting, flashing, and troubleshooting mobile devices. Later, I developed a spreadsheet-based invoice generation tool for my father's ice cream franchise business using LibreOffice Calc and LibreOffice Basic. What started as an attempt to automate a repetitive task turned into a tool that has generated and printed over 2,000 invoices in the past two years and is still actively used today.

These experiences, which I pursued alongside my school education, helped me develop a genuine interest in technology, automation, and software development. Now, I am pursuing a B.Tech in Information Technology at Madhav Institute of Technology and Science (MITS), Gwalior, where I look forward to expanding my knowledge and exploring new technologies.

Currently, I am learning the fundamentals of web development, including HTML and CSS, and have built frontend clones of platforms like Zomato and Swiggy as part of my learning process.

I believe this is just the beginning. I look forward to continuously learning, building meaningful projects, solving problems, and turning ideas into practical solutions.
